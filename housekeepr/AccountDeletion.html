Account and Data Deletion Request

At HouseKeepr, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you no longer wish to use HouseKeepr, you can request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data.

How to delete your account and data

Option 1: Delete directly within the app (Recommended)

You can immediately delete your account directly from the HouseKeepr app. This process is automated and immediate.

  1. Open the HouseKeepr app.
  2. Go to the Settings screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap on Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your choice. Your account and personal data will be permanently removed.

Option 2: Submit an email request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request data deletion by contacting our support team.

  1. Send an email to kianniek@hotmail.com from the email address associated with your HouseKeepr account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Please include your full name and the email address used to log in.
  4. Our team will process your request and delete your data within 14 days.

What happens when your account is deleted?

When you delete your account, the following actions occur:

  • Personal Data: Your profile information (name, email, profile picture) is permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Household Data: You will be removed from any Households you joined. If you are the last remaining member of a Household, that Household (including all its tasks, groceries, and history) will also be permanently deleted.
  • Authentication: Your login credentials and authentication tokens are revoked and deleted from Firebase Authentication.

Please note: This action is irreversible. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered.
